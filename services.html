Staffing Solutions for Every Need

From one-day assignments to C-suite searches, we have the expertise and the talent pool to deliver. Explore how TalentBridge works for employers and job seekers.

For Employers

Workforce Solutions Built Around You

Every business has different staffing needs. We offer flexible solutions designed to match -- whether you need help for a week or a decade.

Temporary Staffing

Need to scale your workforce quickly without the overhead of permanent hiring? Our temporary staffing solutions give you access to pre-screened, qualified workers ready to hit the ground running.

Ideal for: seasonal demand spikes, project-based work, employee leave coverage, production surges, and event staffing.

How It Works

  • Share your requirements -- role, skills, timeline, and shift details
  • We source from our active talent pool and screen for fit
  • Workers arrive on-site, ready and briefed on your expectations
  • We handle payroll, workers' comp, and compliance
  • Ongoing check-ins ensure quality and satisfaction

Benefits

  • Average 48-hour fill time for most positions
  • No long-term employment commitment
  • Reduce overtime costs and employee burnout
  • Performance guarantee -- replacements at no extra cost

Direct Hire

When you need to make a permanent hire and get it right the first time, our direct hire service delivers. We manage the full recruitment lifecycle so your team can focus on what they do best.

Ideal for: mid-level to senior positions, specialized technical roles, management hires, and hard-to-fill positions.

Our Process

  • In-depth job analysis and culture assessment
  • Targeted sourcing through our network, databases, and direct outreach
  • Multi-stage screening: skills, behavioral, and reference checks
  • Candidate presentation with detailed profiles and interview notes
  • Offer negotiation support and onboarding coordination

Timeline & Guarantee

Most direct hire searches are completed within 2-4 weeks. Every permanent placement includes a 90-day guarantee. If the hire does not work out for any reason within that window, we restart the search at no additional cost.

Temp-to-Perm

Not sure if a candidate is the right long-term fit? Our temp-to-perm program lets you evaluate talent on the job before making a permanent commitment. It is the lowest-risk path to building your team.

Ideal for: new roles with evolving requirements, companies with rigorous culture standards, growth-stage businesses testing new positions, and managers who want to see performance before committing.

How Conversion Works

  • Worker starts as a TalentBridge temporary employee
  • Typical evaluation period: 60-90 days (flexible)
  • You assess performance, culture fit, and potential
  • When ready, convert to your permanent payroll with a prorated fee
  • We handle the transition paperwork and benefits coordination

Payroll Services

Already have the people but need help managing the backend? Our employer-of-record and payroll management services handle the administrative complexity so you can focus on your core business.

What We Handle

  • Employer of record (EOR) services for your contingent workforce
  • Weekly and biweekly payroll processing
  • Tax withholding, filing, and compliance
  • Workers' compensation coverage and claims management
  • Benefits administration: health, dental, vision, 401(k)
  • Onboarding documentation and I-9 verification
For Job Seekers

Your Next Opportunity Starts Here

Whether you are actively looking or just exploring what is out there, TalentBridge connects you with real opportunities at companies that value their people.

How to Apply

Getting started is easy. Submit your resume through our contact page or call our office directly. A recruiter will reach out within 24 hours to discuss your background, goals, and what types of roles interest you. No fees, no obligations.

What to Expect

After your initial conversation, we will schedule a skills assessment and in-person or virtual interview. We will discuss available opportunities, walk you through the placement process, and set expectations on timeline and next steps.

Benefits of Working With Us

Access to jobs that are not posted publicly. A dedicated recruiter who advocates for you. Weekly pay with direct deposit. Health benefits on qualifying assignments. And ongoing career support from people who know your name.

Types of Positions

We place candidates at every level -- from entry-level warehouse associates to senior software engineers to executive leadership. Full-time, part-time, and flexible schedules available across all of our industry verticals. Many of our temporary assignments lead to permanent offers.

Industry Expertise

Industries We Serve

Our sector-focused approach means your recruiter speaks your language, understands your compliance requirements, and knows where to find the right people.

Manufacturing
