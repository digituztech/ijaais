Aim & Scope

The International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is an international, peer-reviewed journal committed to advancing research and innovation in the rapidly evolving domains of Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems. IJAAIS serves as a global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners, and policymakers to exchange knowledge, present innovative methodologies, and share impactful solutions addressing both theoretical advancements and real-world challenges.

IJAAIS is dedicated to publishing high-quality scholarly manuscripts that reflect state-of-the-art developments in Applied Artificial Intelligence. The journal welcomes contributions that explore foundational theories, novel algorithms, system architectures, optimization techniques, and domain-specific AI applications. By encouraging both conceptual research and practical implementation studies, IJAAIS aims to bridge the gap between academic research and industrial deployment.

As an open-access journal, all accepted articles are made freely available to the global research community, ensuring enhanced visibility, accessibility, and academic impact. The journal promotes responsible, explainable, and sustainable AI practices that contribute to societal and technological advancement

IJAAIS publishes four regular issues annually in March, June, September, and December, and also features special issues managed by distinguished guest editors focusing on emerging trends and specialized research areas in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems.

The editorial board of IJAAIS is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic rigor, transparency, and research integrity. Through a robust peer-review process and continuous quality enhancement, the journal strives to position itself among leading international publications in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Systems research.

Scope

  • The scope of IJAAIS encompasses a wide range of topics in Applied Artificial Intelligence, Computational Intelligence, and Intelligent Systems Engineering.

The journal welcomes contributions in (but not limited to):

 

Core AI Areas

  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
  • Explainable AI (XAI)
  • Generative AI & Foundation Models
  • Knowledge Representation & Reasoning
  • Multi-Agent Systems

Applied AI Systems

  • AI in Healthcare
  • AI in Agriculture & Smart Farming
  • AI in Cybersecurity
  • AI for Smart Cities
  • AI in Finance & FinTech
  • Industrial AI & Industry 4.0
  • Edge AI & Embedded AI Systems
  • Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Emerging & Interdisciplinary Areas

  • AI Ethics & Governance
  • AI for Sustainable Development
  • Human-AI Interaction
  • AI in Education
  • IoT & AI Integration
  • Digital Twins & Intelligent Infrastructure

Research Priorities

IJAAIS particularly values:

Novel Theoretical Contributions

  • Development of new AI models and algorithms
  • Enhancement of existing frameworks
  • Identification of emerging research challenges
  • Theoretical advancements in intelligent systems

Empirical and Experimental Research

  • Performance evaluation of AI models
  • Benchmarking studies
  • Simulation and system validation
  • Real-world AI deployment case studies

Applied & Practical Contributions

  • AI-based system architecture designs
  • Industry implementation reports
  • Prototyping and deployment frameworks
  • Scalable AI solutions for societal challenges

 

Target audience

  • Researchers, academicians, industry experts, practitioners, and students who are engaged in the study and application of Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems, particularly in understanding how AI models and technologies are conceptualized, developed, implemented, evaluated, and responsibly managed across various sectors.
