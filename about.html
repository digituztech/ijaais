Our Story

Founded to Fix What Was Broken

In 2008, our founder Karen Walsh had spent 15 years in the staffing industry and saw the same problems everywhere: candidates treated as interchangeable parts, clients handed off to junior reps after signing, and a revolving door of mediocre placements that wasted everyone's time and money.

She started TalentBridge with a different model. Every candidate gets a real conversation, not just a skills checklist. Every client gets a dedicated account manager who learns the culture, not just the job description. And every placement is backed by the kind of follow-through that turns a hire into a long-term success.

What started as a two-person operation in Columbus, Ohio, has grown into a regional staffing leader serving over 500 companies across 12 states. The company is bigger, but the approach has not changed: treat people like people, not numbers.

Our Approach

Relationship-First Staffing

We do staffing differently. Here is what that actually looks like in practice.

Deep Discovery

We start every engagement with in-depth conversations -- not intake forms. We learn your culture, your pain points, and what success actually looks like on the ground. For candidates, we go beyond skills to understand career goals, work style, and what matters most to them in a role.

Thorough Screening

Every candidate goes through a multi-step screening process: skills assessment, behavioral interview, reference verification, background check, and drug screening where required. We verify credentials and certifications. No shortcuts, no exceptions.

Ongoing Support

The placement is not the finish line. We check in regularly with both clients and placed workers during the first 90 days and beyond. If something is not working, we address it fast. Our goal is long-term success, not a quick transaction.
