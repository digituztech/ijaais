Article Processing Charges (APC)

The International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is an open-access journal committed to ensuring that published research is freely accessible worldwide without subscription barriers.

To support editorial management, peer review coordination, formatting, hosting, and digital archiving, an Article Processing Charge (APC) is normally applicable only after acceptance of the manuscript.

 

APC Waiver for 2026

IJAAIS is offering a full waiver of Article Processing Charges for all accepted manuscripts published in 2026.

  • APC for 2026: FREE (No publication charges)
  • No submission fees
  • No hidden charges

This waiver applies to all authors, both domestic and international, for issues published in 2026.

Standard APC Structure (Effective from 2027 onwards)

  • For Authors from India: ₹ 2,000
  • For International Authors: USD 200

APC payment instructions will be communicated only after formal acceptance of the manuscript.

What the APC Covers

The APC includes:

  • Editorial handling and double-blind peer review
  • Plagiarism screening
  • Copyediting and formatting
  • Online open-access publication
  • DOI assignment
  • Digital archiving and indexing support

Refund Policy

  • APCs are non-refundable once the article has been published.
  • In cases of withdrawal prior to publication, refund decisions are subject to publisher discretion.
