IJAAIS

Ethical Guidelines for Journal Publication

The International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is committed to maintaining the highest standards of publication ethics and academic integrity. The journal adheres to the principles and Code of Conduct established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

IJAAIS expects ethical behavior from all parties involved in the publication process, including Authors, Editors, Reviewers, and the Publisher. Ethical compliance is essential to uphold transparency, research credibility, and scholarly excellence.

Conformance to these standards is mandatory for all submissions and publication processes.

Responsibilities of Authors

Authors submitting manuscripts to IJAAIS must adhere to the following ethical principles:

  • Present an accurate, objective, and comprehensive account of their research, including sufficient methodological details to enable reproducibility.
  • Ensure that all submitted work is original. Proper citation and acknowledgment must be provided when using the work or ideas of others.
  • Avoid plagiarism in any form, including self-plagiarism and improper paraphrasing.
  • Refrain from submitting the same manuscript to multiple journals simultaneously.
  • Avoid publishing substantially similar research in more than one journal.
  • Disclose any potential conflicts of interest.
  • Ensure that all co-authors have significantly contributed to the research and have approved the final manuscript prior to submission.
  • Take full responsibility for the integrity, accuracy, and authenticity of the research findings.

Fraudulent, fabricated, or knowingly inaccurate statements constitute unethical behavior and are strictly unacceptable.

Responsibilities of Editors

Editors of IJAAIS are responsible for

  • Evaluating manuscripts solely based on academic merit, originality, clarity, and relevance to the journal’s scope.
  • Ensuring a fair, unbiased, and confidential peer-review process.
  • Protecting the confidentiality of submitted manuscripts and author information.
  • Avoiding conflicts of interest in editorial decision-making.
  • Not using unpublished materials for personal research without explicit written consent from the authors.
  • Acting promptly and transparently in cases of suspected misconduct, including plagiarism, data fabrication, or ethical violations.
  • Taking appropriate corrective actions such as corrections, retractions, or expressions of concern when necessary.

Final editorial decisions are made independently and are not influenced by commercial or external interests.

Responsibilities of Reviewers

Reviewers play a critical role in maintaining publication quality. They are expected to:

  • Treat all manuscripts as confidential documents.
  • Conduct reviews objectively and provide constructive, evidence-based feedback.
  • Avoid personal criticism of authors.
  • Decline review invitations if unqualified or unable to provide a timely review.
  • Disclose any conflicts of interest arising from competitive, collaborative, or personal relationships.
  • Refrain from using unpublished data or ideas obtained during peer review for personal advantage.

Confidentiality and integrity in the review process are paramount

Handling Misconduct

In cases of suspected ethical violations, IJAAIS will:

  • Follow COPE flowcharts and guidelines
  • Conduct fair and confidential investigations
  • Allow authors to respond to allegations
  • Issue corrections, retractions, or notices as necessary
  • Notify institutions if serious misconduct is confirmed

Commitment to Academic Integrity

Through rigorous peer review, transparent editorial practices, responsible AI governance, and adherence to international ethical standards, the International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is committed to fostering trustworthy, high-quality, and impactful research in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Statement

The International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of authors, reviewers, and editors.

  • Personal information such as names, email addresses, affiliations, and contact details provided to the journal will be used exclusively for editorial and publication purposes.
  • This information will not be shared with third parties or used for commercial purposes.
  • Manuscripts submitted to IJAAIS are treated as confidential documents.
  • All participants in the editorial and review process are expected to maintain strict confidentiality.

Evaluation Process : Double-Blind Peer Review

IJAAIS follows a Double-Blind Peer Review system to ensure fairness, objectivity, and academic integrity. Our journal maintains an acceptance rate of approximately 30%, ensuring a rigorous and selective peer review process.

The evaluation process consists of two main stages:

1. Desk Review

Upon submission, manuscripts undergo an initial editorial screening (desk review) conducted by the Editor-in-Chief or assigned Academic Editor.
During this stage:

  • Author identities are concealed.
  • The manuscript is assessed for:
    • Relevance to the journal’s scope.
    • Originality and novelty.
    • Academic rigor.
    • Compliance with submission guidelines.
    • Ethical standards and plagiarism screening.

Where necessary, members of the Editorial Board or Scientific Committee may be consulted.
Manuscripts that do not meet the journal’s scope or quality standards are rejected at this stage. Authors are typically notified within 4–6 weeks of submission.
Manuscripts that pass the desk review proceed to the peer-review stage.

2. Double-Blind Peer Review

Approved manuscripts are forwarded to at least two independent expert reviewers with relevant expertise in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems.

  • Both authors and reviewers remain anonymous.
  • Reviewers are selected based on subject expertise and absence of conflicts of interest.
  • Reviewers are typically given 10–21 days to complete the review.

The evaluation focuses on:

  • Relevance and innovation of the research topic
  • Originality and contribution to AI and systems research
  • Clarity and quality of writing
  • Soundness of theoretical framework
  • Appropriateness of methodology
  • Depth and rigor of analysis
  • Validity of results and conclusions
  • Quality and relevance of references

The peer-review stage may take up to 8–12 weeks, depending on reviewer availability.

Revision Process

If revisions are requested:

  • Authors may be given 30–60 days to submit a revised manuscript.
  • Revised manuscripts may be returned to the original reviewers for reassessment.
  • Authors must provide a detailed response to reviewer comments.

Peer Review Policy

Reviewer Selection

The Academic Editor determines whether additional expert opinions are required beyond the standard two reviewers.

Reviewer Confidentiality

Reviewer identities remain anonymous unless a reviewer voluntarily chooses to disclose their identity.

Number of Reviewers

Most manuscripts are evaluated by two external reviewers, though additional reviewers may be assigned if necessary.

Editorial Decisions

The final decision on a manuscript is made by the Academic Editor based on reviewer recommendations and editorial assessment.

Possible Decision Outcomes

After evaluation, one of the following decisions will be issued:

  • Accept
  • Minor Revision
  • Major Revision
  • Reject

IJAAIS is committed to a transparent, fair, and rigorous review process that ensures the publication of high-quality research in Applied Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems.
