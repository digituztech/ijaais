Our Mission

The mission of IJAAIS is to disseminate high-quality, impactful, and ethically grounded research in Applied Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems across diverse domains.

The journal is committed to:

  • Advancing AI-driven innovations that address real-world challenges.
  • Promoting interdisciplinary AI research.
  • Supporting research contributions from both developed and developing nations.
  • Encouraging responsible, explainable, and sustainable AI practices.

Our Goals

The main goals of IJAAIS are:

  • To promote scientific excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Systems research.
  • To publish original and unpublished research contributions.
  • To foster collaboration between academia, industry, and research institutions.
  • To encourage AI applications that contribute to societal development and sustainability.
  • To provide a rigorous peer-review platform ensuring academic integrity and quality.

Advancing Innovation

The International Journal of Applied Artificial Intelligence and Systems (IJAAIS) is a peer-reviewed, international scholarly journal dedicated to advancing research and innovation in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems. The journal serves as a global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and policymakers to publish high quality theoretical, empirical, and applied research in AI and its interdisciplinary applications.

IJAAIS promotes cutting-edge research that bridges the gap between foundational AI theories and real-world system implementation.
