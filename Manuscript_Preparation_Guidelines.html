Manuscript Preparation Guidelines

File Format

Authors must submit manuscripts in Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) format

  • Tables, figures, photographs, and graphs (color or black & white) must be electronically embedded within the main text at appropriate positions.
  • All images and graphical elements should be of high resolution (minimum 300 dpi).
  • The manuscript must have 2.54 cm (1 inch) margins on all sides.
  • The document should be formatted in Times New Roman font throughout.

Manuscript Title

  • The title should appear at the top of the first page.
  • Font: Times New RomanTables, figures, photographs, and graphs (color or black & white) must be electronically embedded within the main text at appropriate positions.
  • Size: 14 points
  • Style: Bold, ALL CAPITAL LETTERS
  • The title should be concise, informative, and reflect the core contribution of the research.

Author Names

  • List the corresponding author first, marked with an asterisk (*).
  • Format: Last Name followed by Initials
  • Font size: 12 points
  • Style: Bold

Institutional Affiliation

  • Provide full institutional address for each author
  • Include department, institution name, city, state (if applicable), and country.
  • Font size: 12 points

E-mail Address

  • Provide the email address of the corresponding author
  • Font size: 10 points
  • The corresponding author is responsible for all communication with the journal.

Page Numbering

  • Pages must be numbered consecutively
  • Page numbers should appear at the bottom center or bottom right

Manuscript Structure

The manuscript should be arranged in the following order

  • Title Page
  • Abstract
  • Keywords (4–6 keywords recommended)
  • Introduction
  • Related Work (if applicable)
  • Materials and Methods / Methodology
  • Results
  • Discussion or Results and Discussion
  • Conclusion
  • Acknowledgements (if any)
  • Funding Information (if applicable)
  • Conflict of Interest Statement
  • References

Figures and Tables must be appropriately placed within the text near their first citation and properly numbered.

 

Figures and Tables

  • Must be clearly numbered (Figure 1, Table 1, etc.).
  • Each figure and table must have a descriptive caption.
  • Ensure that all figures are legible and of publication quality.
  • Avoid duplication of data between tables and figures.

References

  • References must be listed on a separate page at the end of the manuscript under the heading “References.”
  • All references must follow the APA
  • References should be arranged alphabetically by the last name of the first author.
  • In-text citations must follow the author date format, for example
    • Single author: (Smith, 2025)
    • Two authors: (Smith & Kumar, 2023)
    • Three or more authors: (Ramanathan et al., 2024)
  • Every reference cited in the text must appear in the reference list, and all references in the list must be cited in the manuscript.
  • Authors are responsible for ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and proper formatting of all references.
  • DOI numbers must be included whenever available and presented as active URLs.
